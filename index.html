Each method uses the same saved camera images. The first scroll leaves the whole-body view. The lung detail keeps 21 sampled frames. Every frame from F0956 through F1084 stays in the cut.

The live view uses the registered rib and diaphragm motion, airway masks, and particle paths for the selected camera. Breathing fades at the source camera’s close-up cutoff. Choose Camera plates in Settings to compare the saved images alone.

The speed test sweeps the lung and airway at 0.25 to 16 screens per second. If 16 passes, it tries 32, then 64 if 32 passes. A rate passes when each shot shows at least four camera changes, stays within 0.20 screens of the requested frame for 95% of the trial, has animation callback gaps at or below 50 ms for 95% of the time, and settles within 200 ms.
